About This Universe

An Alternate History Built in WWE 2K26

This is an independent, fan-created WWE 2K26 Universe Mode project. Our fictional timeline begins January 1, 1984, with Vince McMahon's WWF unifying the major wrestling territories. In this alternate universe, the promotion remains known as WWF. Matches are played in the game; outcomes and rankings are tracked as the story unfolds.
