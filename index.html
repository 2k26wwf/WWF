About WWF Universe

This is an independent, fan-created WWE 2K26 Universe Mode alternate-history archive beginning January 1, 1984. In this fictional timeline, Vince McMahon unifies the major territories into the WWF, and WWF never changes its name. Confirmed match results come from gameplay; unplayed matches and proposed stories are identified as such. This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WWE, 2K, or any rights holder. Trademarks and character likenesses remain the property of their respective owners.
